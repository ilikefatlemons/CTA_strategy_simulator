## 1.6 — Futures contract splicing (DEFERRED per the doc)

The commodity tickers (USO/GLD/SLV) are **ETF proxies, not futures** — Alpaca has no
futures data. When real continuous-contract futures are wired in, build BEFORE any
futures result is trusted:
- roll rule fixed in advance (calendar or volume/open-interest crossover), never
  selected by backtest outcome;
- rolls modelled as real trades — two spreads + two commissions per roll;
- signals suppressed across the splice (no pattern formed from bars of different
  contracts);
- adjustment method declared with its known distortion (back-adjusted preserves
  dollar moves but breaks % returns and price levels; ratio-adjusted preserves %
  returns but breaks dollar levels).

Pointer: DEPLOYER doc §7 "1.6 — Futures contract splicing".
